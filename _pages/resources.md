## Data status

Google Scholar is used to identify the researchers and link their public profiles. Joint-publication entries currently come from the maintained bibliography in the supplied reference repository and are cross-checkable through DOI links. Scholar does not provide grant amounts, advising relationships, awards, or complete email addresses; those fields require institutional or funder sources.
